## Verification Debt

- Japan destination-side authority is not wired cleanly to fixed-departure rows: licensed local guides are findable, but operators rarely name the exact assigned guide on the public product page.
- All Japan Tours, Walk Japan, and InsideJapan remain capped as CLAIMED for guide expertise because the guide/leader is described by role, not named and independently corroborated.
- JP-01 is a useful ranking, but not a converged country/theme proof. It is the first non-Western bias test and it shows the method needs a separate day/private local-master ranking path or a stronger way to bind local licensed guides to products.
