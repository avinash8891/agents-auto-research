## PANEL 2 — output: quality / anti-hallucination / global-bias / adversarial-marketing / cost / robustness
**Counts:** {'blocker': 12, 'major': 19, 'minor': 14}

**Verdict:** NO — as written, this playbook will NOT reliably produce genuinely-best, non-invented, globally-fair, affordable tour rankings. It is excellent at one thing and one thing only: proving that a tour EXISTS and was not invented by the agent. On all three of the deliverable's actual promises it fails. (1) GENUINELY-BEST: the method verifies existence, never quality — "highly regarded" and "depth" (ranking criteria 1 and 2) have no evidence source, so a glossy operator that fills in the fields ranks identically to a superb one. (2) NON-INVENTED: airtight against AGENT invention, but wide open to OPERATOR deception — "verified" means "present on the seller's own page," so any SEO-savvy operator that prints "Dr. X, archaeologist, intimate small-group" clears the same bar as a real expert; credential-fit is an unconstrained LLM vibe with no lookup table, and snippet-harvested guide/price strings can reach a ranked row. (3) GLOBALLY-FAIR: the two anti-false-convergence axes (authority-index, language) and the expert-credential gate are all Western/European-seeded, so for the non-Western majority of the 50 it under-finds local operators, mis-scores lineage/indigenous masters as "generic guides," and declares false convergence on Western-facing directories. The entire method is proven on ONE Latin-script Western country (Italy). Affordability is unmanaged, not merely unmeasured — loop-until-dry is unbounded, sweeps re-mine global directories per-country, and every cross-country promotion dirties all prior countries. README "Status" must change from "proven, ready to scale" to "proven for anglophone + Western-European Latin-script rich markets only." Do NOT run the global pilot (Turkey/Mexico/Thailand/Japan in country 1) until clusters C1, C2, C3, C4 are done.

**Biggest risk:** CERTIFIED CONFIDENCE IN A SYSTEMATICALLY-BIASED, UNVERIFIED-QUALITY RESULT. The single deepest root issue is that "verified" is conflated with "true and best," when it actually means only "found on the seller's own marketing page and not invented by the agent." Three independent failure modes compound on this one assumption: (a) quality is never measured (existence-verification masquerades as quality-verification), (b) the seller's own claim is the sole evidence (no independent corroboration of credential, who-leads, or group size — the marketer's optimization target IS the evidence the method trusts), and (c) the discovery frame that decides what gets considered at all is Western/English-indexed (authority-index + language axes + credential model), so the candidate set is biased before ranking even begins. The result is a confident, audit-"healthy," cited ranking that is reliably the most-marketed/most-discoverable tour — and the playbook's own 17-class self-audit is structurally blind to this because every issue-class is about process integrity, none about output quality or selection bias. A wrong answer wearing a verification badge is more dangerous than an obviously-incomplete one.

### Lens verdicts
- **Output quality &amp; selection bias — will the method surface the genuinely BEST expert-led tour, or only the most discoverable/well-marketed/English-indexed?**: As written the method reliably surfaces the most DISCOVERABLE, best-MARKETED, English/Western-indexed expert-led tour and certifies it as 'best' — its verification proves existence not quality, its most productive discovery sources are Western-outbound directories, its convergence equates 'stopped finding' with 'field complete,' and its sole proof is one Latin-script Western country; genuine global 'best/authentic' is not yet attainable without the redo/fixes above.
- **Anti-hallucination & verification rigor: is the no-invention guarantee airtight against fabricated/over-trusted guide names, credentials, prices, dates, or "expert-led" claims reaching a ranking?**: The no-invention chain is rigorous on STATUS (UNVERIFIED flags, snippet-can't-be-promoted, memory invariant, dirty-propagation) but has two airtight-breaking holes for DISPLAYED content: 'credential fits the lens' is asserted with no lookup table to check against, and snippet-harvested guide/price strings can surface as ranked specifics — plus a stale orphaned SEARCH_PROTOCOL and a proof-of-concept that both model the looser pre-foundation standard the method warns against.
- **Global &amp; cultural bias (deliverable spans France/Italy plus Mexico, Turkey, Japan, Thailand, Egypt, India, Kenya, etc.)**: REDO the expert definition and FIX the authority/channel/language axes: as written, the method's two false-convergence-breaking axes (authority-index, language) and its expert-credential gate are all Western/European-seeded, so for the non-Western majority of the 50 it will systematically under-find local expert-led operators, mis-score indigenous/lineage masters as 'generic guides', and declare false convergence on Western-facing directories.
- **Adversarial / gaming resistance: distinguishing a real credentialed expert from SEO/marketing claims (fake/thin credentials, figureheads who do not actually lead, "small group" that is not).**: The method rigorously prevents the AGENT from inventing experts, but has almost no defense against an OPERATOR marketing fake/thin credentials or a figurehead who does not lead: 'verified' is defined as 'present on the seller's own page,' so any SEO-savvy operator that prints a name, a title, and 'intimate small-group' clears the same bar as a genuine expert — the central anti-gaming guard (credential corroborated independently of the seller, and the named expert confirmed on the actual departure) is missing and should be added as a redo, not a patch.
- **Cost & efficiency at scale (per-country and across 50–150 countries): where is the token/agent/time waste, is loop-until-dry affordable, what is the cheapest path that still meets the never-invented / expert-led / verified quality bar.**: The method is correctness- and convergence-rigorous but cost-blind: loop-until-dry is affordable for the PILOT (10 countries) yet unbounded at 50-150 because saturation is re-run per-theme, authority/language sweeps are re-mined per-country, and every cross-country promotion dirties all prior countries — fix by making sweeps rank-aware, caching operators/directories globally, and adding explicit cost dials, none of which lower the never-invented/expert-led quality bar.
- **Failure modes & robustness — graceful degradation at COUNTRY level (thin/no expert-led market, source down, junk search, theme &lt;2 products, non-converging discovery), stop conditions, budgets, and honest "could not do this well" reporting.**: The method engineers theme-level degradation rigorously but has NO country-level 'we could not do this well — here is why' verdict, no discovery budget/non-convergence stop, no source-down or junk-saturation handling, and a Western-market-biased source/example base — so its claim of graceful global degradation is asserted (one Kenya sentence) rather than built, and a thin/junky non-European country would today emit an unmarked near-empty 'DONE' indistinguishable from full success.
