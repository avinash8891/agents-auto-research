# Italy — Theme Map v0 (pre-discovery)

Rule: non-overlapping by THEME (the tour product), anchored to a region. A region with two genuinely distinct lenses (e.g. Tuscany = Renaissance art AND wine/food) gets two themes — different tours, no double-count. Every region covered. Each theme = one trip, 7–14 days (all <21).
Strength = how deep the *expert-led* tour market is. Discovery loop refines this.

## CENTRE
- **IT-01 Rome & classical antiquity** (Lazio) — imperial/pagan Rome: Forum, Palatine, Colosseum, Ostia, Tivoli. *Strong.*
- **IT-02 Christian & papal Rome** (Vatican / early Christian) — St Peter's, Sistine, catacombs, early-church Rome. Religion lens, distinct product from IT-01. *Strong.*
- **IT-03 Florence & Tuscan Renaissance art** — Florence, Siena, Arezzo, San Gimignano; Uffizi, the masters. *Strong.*
- **IT-04 Tuscany wine & food** — Chianti, Montalcino (Brunello), Val d'Orcia. Food/wine lens, separate from IT-03. *Strong.*
- **IT-05 Umbria — St Francis & medieval hill towns** — Assisi, Spoleto, Orvieto. Spiritual/medieval. *Medium.*
- **IT-06 Etruscan Italy** (S. Tuscany + N. Lazio) — Tarquinia, Cerveteri, Volterra, Chiusi. Pre-Roman archaeology; operators sell standalone. *Medium — discovery to confirm.*

## SOUTH
- **IT-07 Naples, Pompeii & Bay of Naples archaeology** — Pompeii, Herculaneum, Vesuvius, Naples Archaeological Museum, Paestum, Capri. *Strong.*
- **IT-08 Amalfi Coast & Capri** (scenery/leisure) — coastal, distinct from IT-07 archaeology. *Thin on expert-led depth — likely leisure, flag.*
- **IT-09 Puglia & Matera** — trulli (Alberobello), Matera sassi, Baroque Lecce, Salento. *Medium.*
- **IT-10 Calabria & Magna Graecia** — Greek south: Riace bronzes (Reggio), Locri, Sybaris; rugged coast. *Thin/emerging — discovery to test.*

## ISLANDS
- **IT-11 Sicily — layered civilisations** — Greek (Agrigento, Syracuse), Roman (Villa Romana del Casale), Norman/Arab (Palermo, Monreale, Cefalù), Baroque (Val di Noto), Etna. *Strong; may SPLIT (see loop).*
- **IT-12 Sardinia — Nuragic prehistory & coast** — Nuraghe (Barumini), Bronze-Age civilisation, coast. *Thin/niche — discovery to test.*

## NORTH
- **IT-13 Venice & the Veneto — art & lagoon** — Venice, Palladian villas, Verona, Padua, lagoon islands. *Strong.*
- **IT-14 Emilia-Romagna food** — Bologna, Parma, Modena (Parmigiano, prosciutto, balsamic, fresh pasta, Ferrari). *Strong.*
- **IT-15 Piedmont — Barolo, truffles & Slow Food** — Langhe, Alba truffles, Barolo/Barbaresco, Turin. Distinct food region from IT-04/IT-14. *Strong.*
- **IT-16 Milan & contemporary design/fashion** — design, Last Supper, La Scala, fashion. Design lens (cf. Tokyo). *Medium.*
- **IT-17 Italian Lakes** — Como, Maggiore, Garda; villas & gardens. *Thin on expert-led depth — leisure, flag.*
- **IT-18 Dolomites & South Tyrol** — alpine nature, hiking, Ötzi/Bolzano, Austro-Italian culture. Nature lens. *Medium.*
- **IT-19 Cinque Terre & Liguria** — Genoa, Portofino, coastal hiking. *Thin on depth — leisure/walking, flag.*

## MINOR / WATCH (coverage, likely fold or drop)

- Abruzzo — national parks, bears/wolves wildlife. Niche nature.
- Lombardy lakes/Brescia, Friuli wine — minor.

## Discovery loop (how themes change)
1. Run exhaustive 8-channel sweep per theme.
2. If a theme has a deep, distinct expert-led market the seed missed → **add/split** (e.g. Sicily may split into IT-11a Greek Sicily / IT-11b Arab-Norman Sicily if operators sell them separately).
3. If a theme has no real expert-led depth → **demote to leisure or fold** (likely IT-08, IT-17, IT-19).
4. If two themes' tour products are really the same → **merge**.
5. Repeat until a sweep adds nothing new = no FOMO. Then rank each surviving theme's Top 5.
