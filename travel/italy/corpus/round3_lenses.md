# Round 3 — Adversarial Hunt for Missing Experience Lenses (Italy expert-led tours)

Method: real WebSearch + WebFetch across multiple angles. A lens is reported as a GENUINE NEW THEME only if >=2 credentialed expert-led products (named scholar/guide/artisan) with real URLs were found, AND it is not already covered by the existing 29 themes. Borderline/thin lenses listed at the bottom as TESTED.

Existing-theme overlap was checked: Gardens are NOT "Lakes villas&gardens" (that theme is the Lake-villa estates as places; gardens-as-scholarly-discipline spans Tuscany/Veneto/Rome/Campania and is led by garden historians). Rationalist/Fascist architecture is NOT "Palladio" nor "Milan design" (different period, ideology, and scholarly frame). Galileo science-history is not "Florence Renaissance art." WW1 Alpine front is not "Sicily WWII Husky" (different war, region, front). Jewish heritage is not "Christian/papal Rome."
