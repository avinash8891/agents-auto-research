## Indexes that were MOST productive
1. University alumni travel-partner directories (Cambridge `/all-travel/travel-operators`, Oxford Journeys) — single richest source: yielded Temple World, Distant Horizons, Jon Baines in one curated list, all confirmed scholar-led.
2. Museum travel partners (Met "Travel with The Met" / Museum Travel Alliance / Arrangements Abroad) — yielded the strongest named-curator model (Christiansen, Wolk-Simon) and 140+ museum reach.
3. Royal Academy / National Gallery art-history travel-partner index — yielded Arts & Travel, ArtsAbroad, Art History in Focus.

## Indexes that were DRY (no genuinely-new commercial operator)
- Learned societies (Roman Society / Hellenic Society / Society of Antiquaries): grant-making bodies, not tour operators — no own commercial Italy tours found.
- Wanderlust / Telegraph awards: re-surfaced already-captured names (Peter Sommer, Martin Randall, ACE); only new name via Telegraph axis was Travel Editions (counted above).
- AITO Italy listing page itself: mostly already-captured (Martin Randall, Peter Sommer) + non-cultural (Mountain Kingdoms, On Foot, Airedale Tours, One Traveller — walking/single-traveller, not expert/scholar-led; excluded).

Notes: "UNVERIFIED" = operator + scholar-led model confirmed from authoritative index, but a specific dated 2026-27 Italy departure URL was not pinned during this pass. Nothing invented.
