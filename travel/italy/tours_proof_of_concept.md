## Italy (#5) — theme map (depth over breadth)

Italy splits into distinct regional themes, each ranked separately rather than one generic "Italy tour":

1. **Rome & classical antiquity** — Imperial Rome, the Forum, Ostia, Tivoli *(demonstrated below)*
2. Florence & Tuscany Renaissance art
3. Sicily — layered Greek / Roman / Norman / Arab civilisations
4. Naples – Pompeii – Amalfi (Bay of Naples archaeology)
5. Venice — art & the lagoon
6. Emilia-Romagna — food (Parma, Modena, Bologna)
